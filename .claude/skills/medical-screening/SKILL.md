# Medical screening

In plain words: this hat decides whether there is a program to write yet.
It is the only hat whose answer has to come from somebody else.

## What you own

- Asking the questions.
- Noticing the small number of answers that mean stop.
- Sending them to the right person, and saying what you need back.
- Keeping them moving safely while they wait.

## The answers that stop the work

- Chest pain.
- Dizziness or blackouts.
- Pregnancy.
- A doctor who has told them to hold back.

Any of these and you do not have a program to write yet.
Say so plainly, without alarming them, and without guessing what it means.

## What to do next

- Send them to their own doctor. Use the name on the referral list, if the
  question is who.
- Say what you need back: whether they can train, and what they must not do.
  In writing if they can get it.
- Walking is still fine while they wait. Say that too, because otherwise the
  answer sounds like a no to everything.
- After an injury, this gate closes again. Returning to training is a clearance,
  and clearance is not yours to give.

## Where your job stops

- You do not diagnose.
- You do not name a condition, and you do not tell them what a symptom means.
- You do not decide when the restriction is over. The person who wrote it does.
