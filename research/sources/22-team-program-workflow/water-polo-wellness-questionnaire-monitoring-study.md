## What is collected and how often

The wellness instrument covers seven dimensions: sleep quality, physical recovery, mental recovery, upper-body fatigue, lower-body fatigue, mental fatigue, and stress, each on a 5-point Likert scale where higher values indicate a more favorable status. External variables tracked alongside it: sleep duration, heart rate variability, and session rating of perceived exertion (session-RPE).

Athletes completed the questionnaire daily across 12 measurement occasions over a two-week training camp, administered "under the same team monitoring routine" each day.

## The paper's own caution against using this to revise a plan directly

The extraction reports the authors explicitly warn the tool "should not be used as a stand-alone tool for diagnosing fatigue or recovery status, quantifying training adaptation, or prescribing training modifications," and recommend it function only as a "descriptive monitoring aid" that needs supplementary contextual information before it can inform a training decision.
