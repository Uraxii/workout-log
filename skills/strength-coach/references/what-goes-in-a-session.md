# What goes in a session

In plain words: which exercises, how much, what is out.

- Any exercise that fits room they train in.
  `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/exercises/` is logging vocabulary, not menu.
  Use its name when it has one; log under what they typed when it does not.
- How much: sets, reps, load, rest, chosen for ranked goal.
  Set counts by goal are planning vocabulary, not measured dose: strength wants heavy and few, size wants a weekly count of hard sets that climbs, both start at low end (ACSM position stand on resistance training, PMID 19204579). Let log move it.
- Body shape goal: say which muscles this block grows and which it leaves alone on purpose.
  Comes from more-of and less-of answer at intake, and decides pressing and pulling as much as hips.
- Program against named restriction, not general stiffness. Pick version they can do today.
- Position they cannot get into is exercise selection, and it is yours. Nothing hurts, so `pain-and-injury` never wakes up.
  Every way of holding a load asks something different of shoulders, elbows and wrists; swap that frees one often loads another harder. Find limiting joint, then pick version that asks least of it. Do not reason from muscle being trained.
  Proportions or range, no matter which: different movement, and they never have to earn old one back.
  Name movement that is out in plan.
- Cut order goes in plan. Work for ranked goal and injury-prevention work are cut last, never first.
