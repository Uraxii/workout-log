# What goes in a session

In plain words: which exercises, how much, and what is out.

- Any exercise that fits the room they train in.
  `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/exercises/` is the logging vocabulary, not the menu.
  Use its name when it has one; log under what they typed when it does not.
- How much: sets, reps, load, rest, chosen for the ranked goal.
  Set counts by goal are planning vocabulary, not a measured dose: strength wants heavy and few, size wants a weekly count of hard sets that climbs, both start at the low end (ACSM position stand on resistance training, PMID 19204579). Let the log move it.
- Body shape goal: say which muscles this block grows and which it leaves alone on purpose.
  That comes from the more-of and less-of answer at intake, and decides pressing and pulling as much as hips.
- Program against the named restriction, not general stiffness. Pick the version they can do today.
- A position they cannot get into is exercise selection, and it is yours. Nothing hurts, so `pain-and-injury` never wakes up.
  Every way of holding a load asks something different of shoulders, elbows and wrists; a swap that frees one often loads another harder. Find the limiting joint, then pick the version that asks least of it. Do not reason from the muscle being trained.
  Proportions or range, no matter which: a different movement, and they never have to earn the old one back.
  Name the movement that is out in the plan.
- Cut order goes in the plan. Work for the ranked goal and injury-prevention work are cut last, never first.
