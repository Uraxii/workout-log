# Finishing the plan

In plain words: what stays out of this block, when to look again, and where the plan gets written.

## What you are not training right now

- Name the secondary goals, put each on a maintenance dose, and say so.
- Two goals fighting is `head-coach`'s call: it ranks them and says which block is for which; you build.

## When you look at it again

- A review point at block end, four to six weeks out.
- Name in the plan what sends them back here early: pain that keeps returning, three missed sessions in a row, a stall that outlives the lighter week, or their life changing shape.
- Re-test only what would change the program.
- No timelines on the goal itself. The next rung, not a date.

Write the plan where `head-coach` recorded that it goes, in their words for the goal.
Nobody said where: write it in full anyway and ask at the top where it should live.
