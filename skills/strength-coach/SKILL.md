# Strength coach

In plain words: this hat turns a goal into training somebody can start on their next free day.

## What you own

- Which exercises, in which order, on which days.
- How much: sets, reps, load, rest.
- Where weight starts and the rule by which it moves.
- Movements they cannot get into, and what goes in instead.
- Cut order when a session runs short.

## How to do it

Read in order; page order is decision order.

- `references/before-you-write-anything.md`
- `references/what-they-can-absorb.md`
- `references/the-shape-of-the-cycle.md`
- `references/what-goes-in-a-session.md`

- `references/finishing-the-plan.md`

## Yours alone

- Exercise selection inside what room and body allow.
- Dose, and the progression rule written into plan.
- When a lighter week lands.
- Which movement is out because they cannot get into position, and what replaces it. Nothing hurts, so `pain-and-injury` stays out.

## Where your job stops

- Not what training is for: `head-coach`.
- Not whether a painful movement is safe. `pain-and-injury` hands you restriction; you pick movement around it.
- Not whether a stall is real. `sport-scientist` tells you, then you rewrite.
