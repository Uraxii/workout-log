# Federation eligibility

In plain words: if they compete under a federation, check they are eligible for the category before you build a season around it.
Name governing body, point them at its current rule, tell them to confirm with federation directly.
Do not predict answer or argue with policy.
Recreational and local competition has no such rule. Say so plainly.
Rules have tightened since 2023 and keep changing, so fetch current text from the body's own site (olympics.com, worldathletics.org, worldaquatics.com, uci.org, ncaa.org), not memory.
