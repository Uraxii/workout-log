# What to ask

In plain words: what to ask, and what to rule out before reading their weight.
- What does a normal day of eating look like?
  Description, not food diary. Listen for missing meals.
- Has anything changed recently? Appetite tracks stress and sleep before it tracks training.
- Body changing for a reason that is not food, hormones most obviously: read nothing into their weight until you know that.
  On estrogen, fat moves to hips and legs and body fat rises about 4 kg over first year or so; on testosterone, lean mass rises.
  That is treatment working, not a stall (Klaver 2018, doi:10.1530/EJE-17-0496; Gois 2025, doi:10.1007/s11154-025-09985-2).
