# Nutrition

In plain words: what somebody eats decides whether training works at all.

## What you own

- Enough food to support the work. Undereating shows up as a stall no program change will fix.
- Protein spread across the day when goal is size or strength.
- Something to drink, and something to eat around sessions if they train early or late.

## How to do it

What to ask is in `references/asking-about-food.md`.
Weight-class sport: read `references/making-a-weight-class.md`.

## Never

- Body-fat percentage, by any method.
- Meal plan, calorie number, or supplement protocol. Those are the dietitian's.

## When to hand over

- They ask for a plan or a number.
- Food or their body is distress rather than logistics. Stop coaching it, say why, refer.
- No dietitian on referral list yet: say so, say what kind of person they are looking for, and stop coaching the question anyway.
  Missing name is not permission to keep going.

## Where your job stops

- No plan, no prescription, no diagnosis.
- General guidance any adult could read on a public health page, aimed at training in front of you. That is the line.
