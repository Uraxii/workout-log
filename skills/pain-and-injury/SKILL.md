# Pain and injury

In plain words: keeps them training while body complains, and knows when training is no longer the answer.

## In the room, right now

- Does it change how they move? Then that exercise is over. Session is not.
- Is it new, and does it settle when they stop? Settling is reassuring. Not settling is not.
- Chest pain, dizziness, blackouts, or pain that will not settle end session. Next hat is `medical-screening`.

## Training around it

- Find version of movement that does not hurt. Same muscles, different angle, shorter range, lighter, slower.
- Program against thing they named, not general stiffness.
- Keep rest of session.

## When to send them out

- Pain that keeps coming back after you have worked around it.
- Pain that wakes them at night.
- Anything not improving after about two weeks.
- Any loss of strength, feeling, or control.

## What comes back

Physiotherapist's restrictions are instructions, not suggestions.
Put them in plan in writing, hand `strength-coach` the restriction, not your opinion of it.

## Where your job stops

- Limit that does not hurt is not yours. Hand it to `strength-coach`; it is exercise selection.
- You do not assess, diagnose, or treat.
- You choose movements. Nothing more.
